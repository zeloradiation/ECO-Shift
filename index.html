Is my area getting hotter?

Works for anywhere in the world. We get the average air temperature for every year from 1984 to 2025 from POWER, then run our trend test on it.

Press "Use my location", or type a place (it starts on Riyadh) and press Check.

POWER values are for a grid square about 50 km wide, not one thermometer. Your location is only sent to the POWER data service to get the data.

How it decides

For every place we answer 4 questions.

What?

POWER and NOAA give monthly or yearly numbers. The page turns them into one number per year for the months you chose.

How much?

How much = slope of the best-fit line through the yearly values.

Real or noise?

Real or noise = Mann-Kendall test. A trend is called real only if the chance of random noise is under 5%.

Where?

The same test runs on 23 cities, so you can see where the change is real and where it is not.

Data = POWER (MERRA-2 model and CERES satellite data) and NOAA NCEI Climate Data Online (CDO) ground stations.

About us

Who we are and why we made this.

ECO DRIFT was built for the Space Apps Challenge “Be an Earth System Trend Detective!”. We wanted anyone in Saudi Arabia, not only scientists, to be able to ask a simple question about the place where they live: is it really changing?

We take decades of free satellite and NOAA data, run a statistics test on it, and show the answer in plain words on a globe you can spin.

Our mission

Make Earth data easy to understand, honest, and about your own city.

Easy to understand

Plain words and simple charts, no confusing terms.

Honest

If something could just be random, we say so. And we never claim to know the cause.

Open data

Everything comes from free satellite and NOAA data that anyone can check.

The team

Team Zelo Radiation: a team of high school students.
